CloudDeck onboarding, one window for every provider. Spec: CloudDeck — Provider Onboarding Design (Claude doc). The left rail lists every loaded provider with what CloudDeck found for it; the right pane shows that provider's connections, sign-in and scope. Click a provider, click a row, press ↵ (or Open) to go on. Use the controls to jump to any state. Data, accounts and projects are fake.
