CloudDeck main window, 20 instances. Use the toggles for light/dark and live/demo. Profile and region live in the status bar (click to switch). Click rows, click column headers to sort, use ↑/↓ or j/k to move, ⌘F (or /) to focus search, Esc to clear, right-click a row for the context menu. State and type facets in the sidebar are live (counts follow the other filters); pinned searches and recent instances sit below them. Icons are inline 16px / 1.2px-stroke Lucide-style paths.
